## Reachability and economy concerns

Review the source-generation pools and recipes for the wooden die, hollow-log drum, forest map, acorn biscuits and brass bell: the registry lists them, but normal acquisition is not directly established by every loot pool. Count actual long-run spawning and crafting reachability. Rarity is not price: NPC personal value, sentiment and scarcity remain separate. Four v3 vape labels share one eligible mythic pool at Human Trail; v4 introduces five adult-only contraband types through a finite-stock wandering trader, not the 0.5% rarity pool.
