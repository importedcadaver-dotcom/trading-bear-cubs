# The Little Trading Post — Original user concept

**SOCIAL STRATEGY · BARTER ECONOMY · PIXEL ART · SMALL INDIE**

*The cubs have invented their own economy. Their parents have no idea.*

## The premise

You play a young brown bear cub living in a large woodland community.

The local cubs have developed a tradition of trading little treasures: polished stones, feathers, toys, berries, handmade objects, and mysterious things they found while exploring. The most valuable things are human artifacts; the mythical most valuable item is a vape.

You decide to open a trading post.

Initially, you own **three berries and a very nice stick**.

Your goal is to become the community's most successful trader while forming friendships, dealing with rivals, and discovering what the other cubs actually value.

## Signature mechanic: objects have personal value

Different cubs want different things. One treasures a blue feather because it reminds her of her mother. Another will trade valuable supplies for a toy he desperately wants. Objects also have physical uses: a cart transports goods, a handmade toy entertains younger cubs, and shelter protects stock. Values emerge from needs and relationships, not a single fixed market price.

**Other cubs trade independently.**

## Gameplay loop

Find or create goods → observe preferences → negotiate trades → expand the post → respond to competing traders and changing demand.

## Original 30-second example

A cub offers three berries for a carved wooden fish. You refuse. Later, another cub trades that same kind of fish for a rare blue stone. You return to the first cub and offer another toy; he accepts. But the blue stone has already been traded away. The market did not wait for you.

## Progression and consequences

Shop improvements, trading tools, crafting, reputation and influence. Dishonesty creates grudges; generosity builds relationships; rivals undercut, partner up or spread rumors.

Original prototype target: **one clearing, six cubs, ten items, NPC-to-NPC trades and simple barter**.

Original biggest risk: trading that is genuinely interesting without becoming a spreadsheet. Closest earlier idea: *A Very Important Rock*, but this game is a social economy with many useful possessions.
