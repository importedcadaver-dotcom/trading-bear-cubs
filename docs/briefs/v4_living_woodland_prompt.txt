# 6. MAKE THE WORLD'S INTERACTABLES OBVIOUS

Currently, finding items and interacting with the environment feel vague.

Audit each clickable world object.

Implement a consistent interaction-feedback system.

For example, when approaching a berry bush:

- The bush becomes subtly highlighted.
- A label appears: **Berry Bush — E: Gather**.
- The cub reaches toward the bush.
- The bush visibly changes.
- A berry sprite moves toward the inventory.
- A sound confirms success.
- A small notification identifies what was acquired.
- The event appears in the history log.

Use comparable behavior for fishing, collecting stones, opening containers, building, hiding possessions, talking, eating and trading.

Failed actions should explain why:

"Too far away," "Already picked clean," "Need fishing pole," "Pebble isn't home," etc.

Use modest, readable animations rather than overwhelming particle effects.

Make sure notifications do not obscure the character or scene.

Add optional reduced-motion and quiet-feedback settings.

# 10. RARE ORPHANED NEWBORN BROWN BEAR EVENT

Add a very rare, persistent encounter near the human road.

The player may discover a dead mother brown bear and her surviving newborn cub.

Handle this as a consequential, non-graphic event.

The newborn must be a living simulated character, not a generic inventory stack.

Classify the discovery as a **Mythic Encounter**, independently from the ordinary item rarity roll.

Possible player choices:

- Approach and investigate.
- Seek help from Mama.
- Rescue and carry the newborn.
- Bring the newborn home.
- Conceal the newborn from Mama.
- Attempt to care for the newborn.
- Ask another adult bear to help.
- Abandon the encounter, with persistent consequences.

The newborn has age-appropriate needs: warmth, milk, protection, sleep and dependence on adult care.

It must not behave like an eleven-month-old cub.

If the player tries to raise it secretly, implement genuine difficulty and discovery risk.

Mama can react to finding the baby and make her own decisions.

The adult criminal trader may be interested in obtaining the cub illegally, creating a dark moral and social conflict, but the cub should retain character identity and not be reduced to a normal tradable commodity.

The encounter occurs at most once per save unless intentionally reset through cheats.

Avoid making the orphan so rare that the developer cannot test it; provide a force-event command.

# 12. ADULT CRIMINAL WANDERING TRADER

Introduce an autonomous adult bear trader who occasionally travels through the woodland and near the Human Trail.

He operates a secret, illegal business.

He may buy rare human vapes from cubs for money and offer contraband goods, including substances from the attached Bear Drugs encyclopedia.

His existence should create a genuine underground economy, not an unlimited wealth exploit.

## Canon requirements

Use the **frozen Edition 1.0 encyclopedia** faithfully:

- Hookleaf / Nictarine: nicotine-equivalent family; adult-only recreational products, including vapes.
- Skunkmoss / Tetrahum: cannabis-equivalent family, adult recreational use legal and regulated, underage use illegal.
- Brewzol: alcohol-equivalent compound used in multiple beverages.
- Hiveglass: prohibited recreational methamphetamine-equivalent.
- Nectopiates: opioid-equivalent umbrella family, with medical and illicit categories; individual compounds remain unnamed.

Preserve the approved effects, hazards, legal distinctions, and company roles.

Do not invent approved global brands, street slang, drug compounds, prescribing protocols or criminal organizations.

Any new game-local product label must be clearly distinguished from approved encyclopedia canon.

## Trader mechanics

He should have:

- An inventory.
- A route and schedule.
- Limited purchasing power.
- Preferences and prices.
- Secrecy.
- Reputation.
- Potential informants and rivals.
- Actual dialogue.
- Ability to refuse deals.
- Risk of investigation or parental discovery.

Introduce money carefully without destroying the existing barter economy.

Research whether coins should be a limited parallel currency used mainly in human-related and underground trades.

Do not make illicit commerce universally superior to legitimate trading.

Underage access to substances should remain a prohibited, dangerous part of the fictional world, with health, legal and family consequences.

Avoid, synthesis methods, detailed trafficking procedures or risk-free power-ups.

The wandering trader must be an actual character who travels and can be encountered, not a shop window that appears everywhere.
