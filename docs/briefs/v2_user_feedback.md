# v2 — User-directed gameplay expansion (historical request)

Preserve these explicit instructions in later development:

- The mythical vape should be **usable**, not a sealed item with no action; model fictional effects and consequences.
- Add morning and nighttime, eating and drinking, meetings with Mama, hiding suspicious items from Mama, and pride/display around cherished items.
- Audit the current game rather than assuming it works.
- Redraw cub models to look **like bears, not dogs**; research the art style and bear anatomy.
- Fix the exploit of indefinitely spamming "Next Morning" and "Forage" to generate goods without consequences. Prefer meaningful tradeoffs over irritating cooldowns.
- Add more items with real uses: eat berries, build with sticks, and clearly distinguish **carved wooden fish** from edible fresh fish.
- The player **owns the trading post**. Other cubs cannot use the facilities entirely for free by default; implement permissions, fees, rules, construction, seats, tools and amenities, with rival-market consequences.
- More contextual item actions, crafting and meaningful physical objects.
- Relationships beyond commerce, including play and wrestling.
- Siblings, littermates and different cub ages must affect autonomy, dialogue and interactions.

This is a user-requested feature inventory, not proof that every feature was implemented in v2.
