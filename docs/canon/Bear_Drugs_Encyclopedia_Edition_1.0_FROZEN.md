# BEAR DRUGS
## Shared Bear-Civilization Substance Encyclopedia — Edition 1.0

**Status:** FROZEN · **Canon scope:** Five substance families · **Edition date:** 8 November 2026  
**Version rule:** This is the Edition 1.0 baseline. Future families are separate additions, not retroactive edits. Changes to approved canon require a new explicitly authorized revision.

> **Canon key:** **APPROVED** = explicitly chosen or accepted in the project's interviews. **OPEN** = not decided and not to be inferred. **EDITORIAL CLARIFICATION** = resolves an overstatement without introducing new canon.

### Scope and continuity rules

- **APPROVED:** One shared catalog of fictional bear-world substances spans different bear civilizations. Their laws, regional availability, histories and cultures may differ, except where a particular family has a more specific universal rule.
- **APPROVED:** Human drug categories have **invented bear equivalents**, with recognizable effects. The setting also permits genuinely bear-specific substances; no additional bear-only drug is being introduced in this edition.
- **APPROVED:** Both believable drug effects and extraordinary fictional phenomena may exist across the wider project. The five entries below use the approved family-specific level of realism.
- **APPROVED:** Medical, recreational, illegal, stimulant, luxury and traditional/ritual substance categories are within the wider project's scope. Not every category needs a separate family in Edition 1.0.
- **APPROVED:** Names use a natural **mix** of restrained bear-related wordplay, ordinary vocabulary and culturally rooted terms. The setting's scientists do not automatically follow human Latin-style nomenclature.
- **APPROVED:** Substance response *may* vary with bear species, body size, individual physiology, season or product. **OPEN:** Actual differences, clinical doses and species-specific rules; do not invent them.
- **EDITORIAL CLARIFICATION:** The descriptions of effects and harm are fictional parallels to human drug categories; they are not claims that the drugs or these effects have been tested on real bears.

## 001 — Hookleaf / Nictarine

**Core-lore status: COMPLETE**  
**Human parallel:** Tobacco and nicotine  
**Plant/common name:** **Hookleaf** — APPROVED  
**Main active stimulant:** **Nictarine** — APPROVED

**Source and history — APPROVED.** Hookleaf is a cultivated, tobacco-like leaf plant associated with **tropical forest plantations**. Its use began in ancient bear traditions and later became industrialized. The specific appearance of the plant, the etymology of “Hookleaf,” and specific historical peoples or events are **OPEN**.

**Effects and health — APPROVED.** Nictarine is nicotine-like: a short pleasurable buzz, alertness, perceived relaxation, and potential tolerance, cravings, dependence and withdrawal. Individual vulnerability and recovery outcomes vary. Product forms have distinct harms; smoking also entails combustion-related risks.

**Product formats — APPROVED.** Cigarettes and hand rolls; cigars and pipes; disposable vapes, rechargeable devices and cartridges; oral pouches, chewing products and snuff; traditional bear-specific methods; medical cessation aids including patches and related treatments.

**Vape flavor families — APPROVED.** Unflavored/botanical; pine, cedar and forest botanicals; berry/fruit; honey/dessert/sweet; mint/cooling; other regional flavors. Individual flavor product names are **OPEN**.

**Companies — NAMES AND ROLES APPROVED.**

| Company | Approved sector |
|---|---|
| Trailmark Tobacco | Mainstream smoking products |
| Crown Ember | Luxury cigars |
| Northcoil Technologies | Vaping devices/products |
| Denwell Therapeutics | Cessation medicines |

**Law and society — APPROVED.** Recreational Hookleaf/Nictarine products are adult-only across civilizations. **Cub vaping occurs illegally and is a recognized social and public-health problem.** Addiction, treatment, regulation, corporate practices, counterfeiting, taxation and illicit distribution may feature in particular worlds; local laws and institutional details are **OPEN**.

**EDITORIAL CLARIFICATION.** The tropical-plantation source does **not** establish where Northcoil manufactures electronics or Denwell manufactures medicines. Adult-only recreational sales do **not** automatically settle separate rules for supervised medical cessation care in minors.

**OPEN:** Street slang; named cigarettes, vape lines and other products; precise age thresholds; specific company biographies/scandals; plant morphology; precise species responses; individual national laws.

## 002 — Skunkmoss / Tetrahum

**Core-lore status: COMPLETE**  
**Human parallel:** Cannabis / THC  
**Plant/common name:** **Skunkmoss** — APPROVED  
**Main psychoactive compound:** **Tetrahum** — APPROVED

**Effects and health — APPROVED.** Primarily recognizable marijuana-like effects, with realistic risks: possible euphoria, relaxation, laughter, altered perception of time, increased appetite and drowsiness; also possible anxiety, paranoia, impairment of memory/coordination, tolerance and dependence. No extraordinary bear-instinct powers are part of this family's approved baseline.

**Product formats — APPROVED.** Dried flower, joints, pipes and smoking; edibles including honey treats, gummies and baked goods; vaporizers, extracts and concentrates; medical preparations and prescribed products. Named strains, brands and individual medicines are **OPEN**.

**Law — APPROVED.** Adult recreational use is **legal and regulated across all bear civilizations**. Underage use by cubs is prohibited. Specific local rules, ages, medical indications and enforcement practices are **OPEN**.

**Social culture — APPROVED.** **Stigmatized hippie bears and delinquent teenage cubs are described as the most common users** in the project's established social premise. Adult legality coexists with stigma; underage use can be common while remaining illegal.

**EDITORIAL CLARIFICATION.** Do **not** quietly replace “most common users” with “merely the most visible users.” Equally, this fictional social premise does not provide a numeric census, imply that all members of either group use drugs, or justify treating every teen or hippie bear as a user.

**OPEN:** Street slang; strain and product names; dispensary brands; botanical appearance; medical conditions; exact age thresholds; quantitative prevalence; species pharmacology.

## 003 — Brewzol

**Core-lore status: COMPLETE**  
**Human parallel:** Ethanol / alcoholic drinks  
**Intoxicating compound:** **Brewzol** — APPROVED

**Definition — APPROVED.** Brewzol is the alcohol-equivalent intoxicating compound found in **multiple beverage categories**; it is **not** the name of one source plant.

**Beverage families — APPROVED.** Honey-based fermented drinks; berry and fruit wines; grain beers, ales and tavern brews; distilled spirits and strong liquors. Individual drinks, labels, breweries and brands are **OPEN**.

**Effects and health — APPROVED BASELINE.** Recognizable alcohol-like intoxication: relaxation, sociability or lowered inhibitions may occur, alongside worsening judgment, coordination and speech as intoxication increases. Excessive intake can lead to accidents, poisoning and serious health harm; heavy repeated use can result in dependence. Severe withdrawal may require emergency medical care.

**Law and culture — APPROVED.** Brewzol beverages are popular and legal for adults, with regulation, drinking problems, stigma, restrictions and public-health concerns resembling those in human societies. Laws and drinking traditions can vary by civilization.

**OPEN:** Beverage brands/names; exact minimum ages; dates, named historic events and regional traditions; producers; detailed species sensitivity or resistance. No bear species has been declared uniquely resistant to Brewzol.

## 004 — Hiveglass

**Core-lore status: COMPLETE**  
**Human parallel:** Methamphetamine  
**Drug name:** **Hiveglass** — APPROVED

**Effects and health — APPROVED.** Primarily realistic methamphetamine-like stimulant effects: intense euphoria, wakefulness/alertness, appetite suppression and substantial dependence risk. Severe harms may include prolonged sleep deprivation, agitation, paranoia, psychosis, cardiovascular injury, overheating and overdose. Recovery, withdrawal and relapse can feature in stories. No superhuman stimulant ability is canon.

**Law — APPROVED.** Recreational Hiveglass use is prohibited. **Extremely restricted medical applications may exist** in bear civilizations; this is *permission to define such uses later*, not approval of any particular prescription, medicine, diagnosis or institution.

**Social/criminal role — APPROVED.** Both neighborhood-level addiction problems and larger illegal distribution networks exist as possible settings for Hiveglass stories. Exact locations, organizations, manufacturing methods, characters and street names are **OPEN**.

**ADHD and television-inspired story — EDITORIAL CLARIFICATION.** The user suggested that bear ADHD exists, tentatively floated **about 5% prevalence**, and considered a *Breaking Bad*-type bear story. The **5% statistic is not approved**; the precise diagnostic system and any link to medically authorized Hiveglass use are **OPEN**. The proposed crime story is an **idea, not approved character canon**. ADHD is not a synonym for illegal drug use or addiction.

**OPEN:** Criminal groups, producers, production or synthesis methods, street slang, brand names, exact medically authorized uses, exact species reactions, ADHD statistics and any Walter White-style bear character.

## 005 — Nectopiates

**Core-family / umbrella status: COMPLETE**  
**Individual-drug dossiers: NOT YET NAMED OR COMPLETED (OPTIONAL)**  
**Human parallel:** Opioids  
**Umbrella family name:** **Nectopiates** — APPROVED

**Definition — APPROVED.** Nectopiates are a class of opioid-equivalent substances. Some are valuable controlled medicines; others are illicit narcotics. The family as a whole is **not** a single chemical.

**Three distinct member categories — APPROVED.**

1. **Morphine-like medical painkiller** — legitimate controlled analgesic for serious clinical pain; specific compound name **OPEN**.
2. **Heroin-like illicit narcotic** — prohibited, high-risk opioid associated with dependence and illegal distribution; specific compound name **OPEN**.
3. **Very-potent fentanyl-like opioid** — possible tightly restricted medical context plus severe illicit overdose hazard, including misrepresented supply; specific compound name **OPEN**.

**Effects and health — APPROVED BASELINE.** Pain relief, sedation and sometimes euphoria; possible tolerance, physical dependence, addiction and difficult withdrawal. The critical acute risk is **respiratory depression and fatal overdose**. Medical care, emergency reversal treatment and long-term addiction treatment can exist without being a magical instant cure. Physical dependence from legitimate medical use should not automatically be equated with addiction.

**Law — APPROVED.** Authorized medical prescribing is lawful, illicit varieties and unauthorized distribution are prohibited. Specific prescribing rules, penalties, treatment access and health policy vary between civilizations.

**OPEN:** All three member names; individual compounds, brands and preparations; medical protocols, exact age rules, species-specific responses; named trafficking groups; origin stories and histories.

**EDITORIAL CLARIFICATION.** Family 005's “complete” designation applies to its **umbrella-level dossier**. It does not imply that three named individual opioid entries exist.
